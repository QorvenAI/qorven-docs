## Nominative fair use — why logos appear here

Every logo on these docs appears under the legal doctrine of **nominative fair use**. We use brand names and logos only to:

1. Identify the specific service you're connecting to (so you don't confuse Slack with Slacker)
2. Show you at a glance which provider a page covers
3. Never imply endorsement, partnership, or affiliation

If a logo owner prefers we use a text badge instead, email `docs@qorven.ai` and we'll swap to plain text within one business day.

## Report a missing attribution

- **Missing logo owner** — email `docs@qorven.ai`, we'll add within 24h
- **Missing OSS project credit** — open a PR on the [qorven-docs repo](https://github.com/qorvenai/qorven-docs) or email us
- **Trademark concern** — email `legal@qorven.ai`; we prioritise these
