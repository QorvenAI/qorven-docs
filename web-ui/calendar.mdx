The Calendar page shows your upcoming events and lets your agents interact with your calendar on your behalf — checking free slots, scheduling meetings, or blocking time for focused work.

## Supported calendar providers

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Google Calendar" icon="google">
    Connect via OAuth. Agents can read and write all calendars in your account.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Microsoft Outlook" icon="microsoft">
    Connect via Microsoft OAuth (Microsoft 365 or personal accounts).
  </Card>
  <Card title="CalDAV" icon="server">
    Any CalDAV-compatible server — iCloud, Nextcloud, Fastmail, and others.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Connecting a calendar

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Calendar settings">
    Go to **Calendar** in the sidebar, then click **Connect Calendar**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your provider">
    Select Google Calendar, Outlook, or CalDAV. For CalDAV, enter your server URL and credentials.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Authorize access">
    Complete the OAuth flow or credential entry. Qorven stores access tokens encrypted on your server — they never leave.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select which calendars to share">
    Choose which calendars agents can see and write to. You can grant read-only or full read/write access per calendar.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What agents can do with calendar access

Once connected, your agents can:

- **Check availability** — "Am I free Tuesday afternoon?" before scheduling anything
- **Book meetings** — create events with title, time, location, and description
- **Send invites** — add attendees and trigger calendar invitations
- **Find conflicts** — warn you if a proposed time clashes with existing events
- **Block focus time** — create "Do Not Disturb" blocks on request
- **Read upcoming events** — summarize your day or week in a briefing

<Info>
  Agents only act on your calendar when you ask them to, or via an approved scheduled task. They do not autonomously accept or decline invitations.
</Info>

## Upcoming events view

The Calendar page displays your events for the next 7 days in a compact list. Click any event to see its full details. This view is read-only — to make changes, ask an agent or use your native calendar app.

## Permissions

You can revoke calendar access at any time from the Connectors page. When revoked, agents immediately lose the ability to read or write calendar data.
