## The Approvals flow

Some agent actions — sending emails, modifying files, running commands — require your approval before they execute. Approval requests appear as notifications in the dashboard and can also be sent to you via Telegram, Slack, or any connected channel.

You can approve or reject right from the notification, without opening the dashboard. [Learn more about approvals →](/workflows/approvals)
