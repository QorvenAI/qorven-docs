Privacy settings give you control over what data your agents store and what is sent to external AI model providers. Since Qorven is self-hosted, all data stays on your server by default — these settings let you fine-tune that further.

## PII redaction

When enabled, PII redaction strips personally identifiable information from prompts before they are sent to external AI model providers (OpenAI, Anthropic, etc.).

The following are redacted by default when PII redaction is on:

- Email addresses
- Phone numbers
- Credit card numbers
- Social Security / National ID numbers
- Street addresses
- Passport and ID document numbers

Redacted values are replaced with typed placeholders (e.g., `[EMAIL_REDACTED]`) so the model can still understand the structure of the content without seeing the actual PII.

<Info>
  PII redaction applies only to prompts sent to external providers. Data stored locally on your server (memories, audit logs, drive files) is not affected.
</Info>

To enable: toggle **PII Redaction** on and select which categories to redact. You can enable all categories or choose specific ones.

## Memory retention

By default, memories are kept indefinitely. You can set a retention period after which old memories are automatically deleted:

- 30 days
- 90 days
- 1 year
- Never expire (default)

Retention applies to the memory creation date. Memories updated by agents reset their age on update.

<Tip>
  A 90-day retention period is a good balance — agents retain recent context without accumulating stale facts about outdated projects or former clients.
</Tip>

## Data export

Download all your Qorven data as a JSON archive. The export includes:

- All memories (with agent, type, content, and timestamps)
- Audit log entries
- Agent configurations
- Conversation history
- Scheduled tasks

Click **Export All Data** and the archive will be prepared and downloaded. Depending on how long your instance has been running, this may take a minute.

## Deleting memories

To delete all memories for your account: click **Delete All Memories** in the Danger section at the bottom of the page. This is permanent. Agents will lose all retained context and will need to relearn preferences and project context over time.

To delete memories selectively, use the [Memories](/web-ui/memories) page.

## Conversation history

By default, conversation history is stored indefinitely. You can configure automatic deletion of conversations older than a chosen period:

- 30 days
- 90 days
- 1 year
- Keep forever (default)

This setting does not affect the Audit Log, which has its own retention management in [System Settings](/web-ui/settings/system).

<Warning>
  Deleting conversations also removes any memories that were created solely within those conversations and have not been reinforced elsewhere.
</Warning>
